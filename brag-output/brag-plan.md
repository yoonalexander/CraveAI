# CraveAI launch film

20 seconds · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · polished

CraveAI turns natural-language food cravings into nearby restaurant suggestions supported by dish evidence. For people who know what they want to eat but not where to find it, its distinction is matching actual dishes and exposing menu sources.

Angle: from a specific craving to a real menu match, then to a nearby destination. Visual hook: oversized Inter typography and the original CraveAI pin, in the exact sage, charcoal, yellow and off-white project palette.
